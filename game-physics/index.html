01 / GRAVITY

Speed grows during the fall

With air resistance ignored, acceleration is constant: v = g × t.

02 / ENERGY

Height and mass store energy

The impact energy is E = m × g × h. Mass changes energy, but not ideal fall time.

03 / IMPACT

Motion meets the water

The measured fall drives the timing. The splash is a stylized visual response, not a fluid simulation.

HOW THE DEMO WORKS

Physics first, art on top

The falling position uses constant acceleration. The live speed and energy are calculated from the controls. Water spray and ripples are a visual interpretation of impact energy; real water entry also depends on shape, drag, and fluid flow.
