GAMEPLAY AI / PHASER DEMO

Teach enemies to notice and pursue the player.

Approach the forest enemies to trigger their awareness. The Mossback Boar charges when you get close; the Thornling darts after you. Watch their state labels, then use your sword to make space.

THREE CORE IDEAS

What this system teaches

01 / PERCEPTION

Notice the player

A detection radius and vertical check decide when a nearby player is visible to an enemy.

02 / STATE MACHINE

Patrol, chase, recover

Enemy state changes select movement, facing, and animation without mixing their rules.

03 / COMBAT FEEDBACK

Attack with a readable tell

Close-range attack timing, player health, hit reactions, and cooldowns make danger legible.
