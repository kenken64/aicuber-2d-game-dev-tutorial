THREE CORE IDEAS

What this system teaches

01 / SPAWN RULES

Random world pickups

A timer creates pickups ahead of the player while limiting how many can exist at once.

02 / COLLECTION

Contact-based collection

An overlap check collects an item, removes its sprite, and updates the player's counters.

03 / ENEMY DROPS

Rewards after combat

Defeating the grove brute spawns a small random bundle of collectible loot.
